Definitions

Articles
Articles indexed (HAS_MESH) to the descriptor or a narrower descriptor in the expansion with is_major_topic = true. The 40 MeSH check tags (Humans, Female, Aged, ...) are never used.
Expansion
The descriptor plus every narrower descriptor reached by HAS_BROADER_ANCESTOR, which follows MeSH tree numbers (PubMed-style explode).
Design class
One class per Article, the first that matches in this order, from NLM publication types: Guideline (Guideline, Practice Guideline); Systematic review or meta-analysis (Systematic Review, Meta-Analysis, Network Meta-Analysis); Randomized controlled trial (Randomized Controlled Trial, incl. Veterinary); Other clinical trial (Clinical Trial, Clinical Trial Phase I-IV, Controlled Clinical Trial, Pragmatic Clinical Trial, Equivalence Trial, Adaptive Clinical Trial, Clinical Trial Veterinary); Observational study (Observational Study, incl. Veterinary); Other (everything else, including Clinical Trial Protocol and no types).
Primary studies
Articles in the Randomized controlled trial or Other clinical trial class.
Cited / not cited
A primary study is cited when at least one Article anywhere in the graph whose publication types include Guideline, Practice Guideline, Systematic Review, Meta-Analysis or Network Meta-Analysis cites it (CITES edge). An Article citing itself is not counted. Only citations between Articles in the graph are counted, so cited counts are lower bounds: a study not cited within the graph may be cited elsewhere.
2022-2026
The Article's publication year (integer `year`) from 2022 to 2026 inclusive.
Population
All Articles, including identity-only Articles (in this release they have no publication types, MeSH or CITES edges, so they do not enter MeSH-based maps).
